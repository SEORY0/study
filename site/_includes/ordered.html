{%- comment -%} Sets `notes_in_order`: every note in map order, subject by subject in the order of site/_data/study.yml, then by each note's `order`. A note is a page under studies/ and its subject is its folder: studies//.md. Jekyll rebuilds a page's fields on every lookup, so each page is read once into a plain hash (map: "to_liquid") and every loop over the notes reads those; one grouping pass then splits them by folder. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign notes_groups = site.pages | map: "to_liquid" | where_exp: "d", "d.layout == 'note'" | sort: "order" | group_by_exp: "d", "d.path | remove_first: 'studies/' | split: '/' | first" -%} {%- assign notes_in_order = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for s in site.data.study.subjects -%} {%- for g in notes_groups -%} {%- if g.name == s.id -%}{%- assign notes_in_order = notes_in_order | concat: g.items -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- endfor -%}
